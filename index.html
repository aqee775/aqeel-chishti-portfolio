What type of products can you research?

General categories such as Home & Garden, Kitchen, Storage, Cleaning, Pet Supplies, Lighting and Home Improvement. Product eligibility should always be checked before listing.

Do you guarantee profit or sales?

No. Product research can identify opportunities, but profitability depends on live market conditions, costs, competition, fees and execution.

Can you help with eBay listings?

Yes. Listing support can include titles, descriptions, item specifics, product research and structured listing information.

Can you work with my existing workflow?

Yes. Tasks can be organized around the client's preferred process, tools and priorities.
